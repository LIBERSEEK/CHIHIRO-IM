# Marketing Methods for Comment Acquisition

This project adapts a small set of practices from the MIT-licensed `social` and `product-marketing` skills in Corey Haines' Marketing Skills repository. This is a Chihiro-specific summary, not a copy of the upstream skill files.

## Apply to campaign preparation

- Establish product/service, audience, concrete value, and desired conversation before selecting keywords.
- Prefer verbatim customer language and narrow audience signals over generic trend terms.
- Keep a clear boundary between facts provided by the user and hypotheses that still need confirmation.

## Apply to each comment

- Write the way someone sounds after watching and scanning the comments: one spoken sentence, one concrete detail, and a little feeling. Finish the thing people are actually talking about. Speak in short breath units, use one particle when the feeling needs it, put your own experience on 我 and the person in the thread on 你, and show a small limit when you share.
- When someone asks how this video was made, answer with the tool they asked about. When someone asks how it is done, what it costs, whether it holds up, or how they do it themselves, answer from experience and attach the card's product with one spoken frame: “我用过的”, “这个”, “我用的”, or “真心推荐这个”. A stiff formal name is shortened; a site is spoken as “站”. A scene, joke, plot, or step stays on that scene, joke, plot, or step.
- Say one stated fact in full words only when that topic is already being asked: high cost is high call cost, a banned account is a banned or limited account, and a degraded model is a model whose answers got worse.
- Keep the comment inside the stated facts. Prices, outcomes, and proof stay within what the user already gave.
